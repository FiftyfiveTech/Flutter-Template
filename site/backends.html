06 / Infrastructure

Swap your backend without rewriting your app.

REST / Dio

Dio Client Datasource

Sends and receives JSON data from a REST API, using one shared, reusable HTTP client.

Firebase

Cloud Firestore Datasource

Reads and writes data directly to Firestore, without your business logic needing to know it's there.

    ref.watch(baseApiClientProvider),
